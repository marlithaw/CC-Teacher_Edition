Culture Camp 10-Day Launch Kit

Audience: Teachers, culture team, leaders

This launch kit reflects the core Matchbook Culture Camp model: the full system is taught on Day 1 and then scaffolded away across the next 9 days. Every day remains a full Culture Camp day. The daily emphasis changes, but the whole routine set stays live.

Daily Non-Negotiable

Every day of Culture Camp includes:

  • Arrival and First Five teaching or reset
  • Full Morning Meeting sequence
  • Live teaching of transitions across the day
  • Hallway coaching in the moment
  • Cafeteria coaching in the moment
  • Recess coaching in the moment
  • Exit and dismissal coaching in the moment
  • Reflection on the weakest routines and a plan to rebuild them tomorrow

10-Day Scaffold Map

Day Adult Scaffold Stance Everything Taught Today Highest Emphasis If It Breaks Evidence To Capture
1 Full adult modeling and heavy narration Entire culture system Students experience the full system from start to finish Stop and rebuild immediately Which routines students can already imitate and which collapse first
2 Strong adult lead with guided student participation Entire culture system Repeating the full system and naming cues clearly Reset faster and with less explanation Entry, voice levels, transitions, and who still needs direct support
3 Adult lead with shorter directions Entire culture system Students begin carrying more of the routine language Rebuild with one precise correction at a time Student follow-through on cues and first-response behaviors
4 Adult cueing with more student execution Entire culture system Transition speed and consistency across spaces Repractice the exact weak move, not the whole day Timing, clean movement, and peer influence on routine strength
5 Adult coaching more than narrating Entire culture system Hallway, cafeteria, and exit consistency Pause and rerun live routines Which routines hold outside the classroom
6 Lighter adult scaffolds; stronger student repetition Entire culture system Student ownership of voice, materials, and readiness Reset in the moment with visible accountability Whether students self-correct before adults intervene
7 Adults begin pushing responsibility outward Entire culture system Repair language, inclusion, and reset recovery Rebuild conflict and recall routines as part of the teaching Peer interactions, regulation recovery, and repair attempts
8 Adults add leadership without reducing fidelity expectations Entire culture system plus houses Houses and identity integrated into the core routine system Stop leadership layering if routine fidelity drops Whether identity builds ownership without weakening structure
9 Adults add celebration and evidence systems Entire culture system plus Friday Cypher prep Recognition through evidence, not popularity Rebuild routines before celebration if needed Which students can explain why recognition is earned
10 Adults test readiness with lighter support Entire culture system with readiness review Near-independent execution and targeted final resets Rebuild the routines that are still too adult-dependent Which routines are launch-ready and which need Week 3 reteach plans

Core Reminder

Do not treat the "highest emphasis" column as "the only thing we teach today." It only names the strongest spotlight for that day. The whole culture system remains in instruction all 10 days.
