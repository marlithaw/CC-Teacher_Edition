Slide 1 · Title & Promise R1, R5

“The end of the day is a handoff — to the buses, to families, to tomorrow. A calm exit is how we keep it safe and keep our own time.”

Look for

  • Students take the framing seriously
  • Less testing once the rationale is honest

If it breaks

  • If cynical: “Who gets stuck when this is chaos? You do.”

Support adult

  • Be honest about real bus/pick-up constraints in your building.

Language support

  • Non-verbal group signals lower the language demand.

Visual: Exit-door icon. Teach at the true dismissal time.

Slide 2 · Regulate R1

“Box breath — four in, four hold, four out. End-of-day energy spikes; we bring it down on purpose.”

Look for

  • Exhale is controlled
  • Visible drop in restlessness

If it breaks

  • Do it with them; do not coach from the front.

Support adult

  • Breathe alongside anyone who cannot settle.

Language support

  • Gesture carries the count.

Visual: Three breath icons with a 4-4-4 count.

Slide 3 · Orient R1, R5

“Pack. Clear your area. Wait for your release. Move when called. The whole thing is under two minutes when we hold it.”

Look for

  • Area cleared without prompting
  • Students wait for the release, not the bell

If it breaks

  • Re-teach the single step that slipped.

Support adult

  • Post the sequence; use a silent release signal per group.

Language support

  • Four labeled icons anchor the routine.

Visual: Four numbered step icons. Use silent group-release signals.

Slide 4 · Connect R2

“With your partner: where does dismissal actually get unsafe — stairwells, doors, the lot — and what would fix it?”

Look for

  • Students name real pinch-points
  • Restating before responding

If it breaks

  • Frame: “Dismissal gets unsafe when ___, so we ___.”

Support adult

  • Circulate; surface the sharpest, most fixable ideas.

Language support

  • Sentence frame supports the response.

Visual: Two-person talk icon. Ninety seconds, then share out.

Slide 5 · Practice R4, R5

“Rehearse the standard: release on signal, travel right, room left clean. Called group only.”

Look for

  • Movement tied to the signal, not the bell
  • Clean room, orderly travel

If it breaks

  • Reset and re-run the same group.

Support adult

  • Position yourself where you can see the whole exit path.

Language support

  • Physical rehearsal is accessible to all.

Visual: Three icons. Rehearse the real exit path.

Slide 6 · Commit R2, R5

“One commitment: name the move, the standard it holds, and who it protects. Specific and observable.”

Look for

  • Commitment is concrete
  • Student can picture the action

If it breaks

  • Push vague ones toward observable behavior.

Support adult

  • Log commitments for Friday recognition.

Language support

  • Frame supplies the structure.

Visual: Check icon. Carry commitments into the Friday celebration.

Slide 7 · Transition R1, R4, R5

“Stand, chair in, travel your route, hand off. We can time it — but a clean handoff beats a fast one every time.”

Look for

  • Controlled travel
  • Every student to the correct destination

If it breaks

  • Rebuild from the line-up point.

Support adult

  • Confirm handoffs; never trade safety for the clock.

Language support

  • Four icons, four words.

Visual: Four numbered step icons. Time it only once the routine is safe.
