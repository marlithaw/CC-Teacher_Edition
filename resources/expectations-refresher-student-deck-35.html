The Three Standards

Safe. Respectful. Responsible.

Safe
Bodies, words, and spaces protected.
Respectful
Listen fully; disagree kindly.
Responsible
Own it; prepare; repair.
Why this worksThese three carry every routine in the building. When one slips, the routine that depends on it slips too — so we tighten the value, not just the rule.

Slide 1 · Title & Promise R1, R5

“Routines slide over time. This is a reset, not a punishment — we sharpen the standard back to where we set it.”

Look for

  • Students accept the reset framing
  • Non-defensive tone

If it breaks

  • Name that drift is normal; the reset is what strong teams do.

Support adult

  • Keep it about the system, never about naming individuals.

Language support

  • Pair “refresher” with a reset icon.

Visual: Reset icon. Frame as pride in the standard.

Slide 2 · Regulate R1

“Three breaths to settle. We reset our bodies before we reset our routines.”

Look for

  • Exhale longer than inhale
  • Class settles

If it breaks

  • Model with them.

Support adult

  • Breathe alongside anyone wound up.

Language support

  • Gesture carries the breath.

Visual: Breath icons.

Slide 3 · Orient R1, R5

“Safe, Respectful, Responsible — give me a real example of each from this week.”

Look for

  • Concrete, current examples
  • All three defined

If it breaks

  • Re-teach the one with weak examples.

Support adult

  • Tie each value to a routine that uses it.

Language support

  • Three labeled icons anchor the values.

Visual: Three value icons matching the schoolwide matrix.

Slide 4 · Connect R2

“With your partner: name one routine that has gotten loose, and which standard would tighten it.”

Look for

  • Students self-identify drift honestly
  • Value linked to routine

If it breaks

  • Frame: “___ got loose; we fix it by being more ___.”

Support adult

  • Keep it about routines, not classmates.

Language support

  • Sentence frame supports it.

Visual: Two-person talk icon. Keep it constructive.

Slide 5 · Practice R4, R5

“We rehearse the routine we named — attention, prepared, voice level — until it is sharp again.”

Look for

  • Quick, quiet response to the signal
  • Materials and voice at standard

If it breaks

  • Reset and repeat the same routine.

Support adult

  • Choose the routine the class named as loosest.

Language support

  • Physical rehearsal is accessible.

Visual: Three icons matching your attention/voice systems.

Slide 6 · Commit R2, R5

“One reset commitment. Name the routine, the move, and the standard it protects.”

Look for

  • Commitments name a specific routine
  • Observable action

If it breaks

  • Coach vague ones toward a visible move.

Support adult

  • Collect for Friday recognition.

Language support

  • Frame gives structure.

Visual: Check icon.

Slide 7 · Transition R1, R4, R5

“The reset only counts if it travels — hallway, lunch, specials. Same standard, every space.”

Look for

  • Students connect the reset to other settings
  • Calm transition out

If it breaks

  • Re-anchor with the three standards.

Support adult

  • Reference the reset in later transitions today.

Language support

  • Four icons, four words.

Visual: Four icons. Reuse the values across every routine.
