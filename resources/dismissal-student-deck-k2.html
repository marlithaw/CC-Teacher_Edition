Slide 1 · Title & Promise R1, R5

“The end of the day is calm, not wild. Watch how we get ready to go home.” Model the whole routine silently first.

Look for

  • Students watch the full model
  • Bodies stay in their spot

If it breaks

  • Model it again, silently.

Support adult

  • Teach this at the real end-of-day spot, at the real time.

Language support

  • Modeling carries it fully.

Visual: Backpack icon. Rehearse at the actual dismissal time.

Slide 2 · Regulate R1

“Smell the flower. Blow out the candle. Three times.” Do it with them every time.

Look for

  • Exhale longer than inhale
  • Shoulders drop

If it breaks

  • If silliness: model silently once, then invite them back.

Support adult

  • Breathe audibly beside any student who cannot settle.

Language support

  • No language demand — gesture carries it.

Visual: Flower and candle icons with matching hand gestures.

Slide 3 · Orient R1, R5

“Pack. Chair in. Voice off. Eyes on me. Show me each one.” Call them one at a time, then all four.

Look for

  • Each part produced on cue
  • All four held while we wait

If it breaks

  • Re-teach one part only.

Support adult

  • Point to the poster instead of speaking.

Language support

  • Four icons, four moves. Zero language demand.

Visual: Four icons. Print as an end-of-day wall poster at child height.

Slide 4 · Connect R2

“When I call your way — bus, walker, or pick-up — you stand and walk to the door. Point to your way.”

Look for

  • Each child knows their own group
  • Students wait until their way is called

If it breaks

  • Point to the group chart.

Support adult

  • Use a color or picture for each group.

Language support

  • Pointing is a complete answer.

Visual: Three group icons matching your building’s dismissal groups.

Slide 5 · Practice R4, R5

“We are going to practice going home. Wait, stand, walk. If it is not calm, we sit and try again.” Actually rehearse it.

Look for

  • Students wait for their own group
  • Calm bodies, not rushing the door

If it breaks

  • “Back to your spot. One more time.” No commentary on individuals.

Support adult

  • Stand at the door, not behind the group.

Language support

  • Physical rehearsal is fully accessible.

Visual: Three icons. Rehearse the actual walk to the door.

Slide 6 · Commit R2, R5

“Pick the one that is hardest for you. Point to it.” Notice it at the real dismissal.

Look for

  • Every student points
  • Honest self-selection

If it breaks

  • Assign one.

Support adult

  • Name it when you see it at dismissal.

Language support

  • Pointing is sufficient.

Visual: Three pictured promises matching the four expectations.

Slide 7 · Transition R1, R4, R5

“Stand. Bag on. Walk. We only go with our own grown-up.” Practice the safe handoff.

Look for

  • Students leave only with the correct adult
  • Calm bodies at the door

If it breaks

  • “Let’s try that again.” Rebuild clean.

Support adult

  • Confirm each handoff yourself. Never rush this step.

Language support

  • Four icons, four words. No timing at this age.

Visual: Four numbered step icons. Safety, not speed.
