Eat. Talk. Move.
Without Chaos.

One connected routine: enter, eat, exit.

Matchbook Culture Camp · Cafeteria · Grades K–201

Routine 1 · 3 Minutes

Entry

Enter at voice 0–1

Get the entry signal from the adult at the door first.

Move directly

Straight to the lunch line or your seat — no clustering at the entrance.

Stow belongings

Bags in the designated place, never blocking walkways.

Cafeteria · K–202

Routine 2 · 20 Minutes

While We Eat

Voice 2 or below

Talk with your table — not across the room.

Stay seated

Move only with permission.

Clean your spot

Small spills cleaned; trays only when your table is called.

Silent hand raise

See it? Echo it. That’s our whole-group reset.

Cafeteria · K–203

Routine 3 · By Table, Never by Crowd

Exit

Voice 0

Shift to voice 0 in the last two minutes.

Dismissal posture

Sit ready. Stand only when your table is called.

Follow the tray route

No crossing other groups.

Form the line

Line at the door, voice 0, wait for the handoff.
