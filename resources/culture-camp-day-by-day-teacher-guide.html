Day-by-Day Teacher Guide

Audience: Teachers implementing Culture Camp

This guide covers only the protected Culture Camp teaching block that begins at 7:45. It does not schedule Morning Entry, First Five, academics, lunch, restroom, recess, re-entry, cleanup, classroom exit, or dismissal. Those routines remain available in the separate Systems & Procedures bundles and follow each grade level’s master schedule.

This guide follows the actual Matchbook Culture Camp model:

  • The full culture system is taught on Day 1.
  • The full culture system stays live on Days 2-10.
  • Culture is the number one priority during these ten days.
  • If a routine breaks, adults stop, reset, and repractice before protecting pacing.

Schedule authority: The 2026–2027 Master Schedule controls the 7:45 start. During Culture Camp, the authored teaching scaffold controls the end: Days 1–3 end at 8:30, Days 4–5 at 8:25, Days 6–8 at 8:15, Day 9 at 8:05, and Day 10 at 8:00.

Master Schedule Alignment

This alignment was verified against both the current 2026–2027 Master Schedule and the canonical Morning Meeting Culture Camp source. The master schedule establishes when Morning Meeting starts. The Culture Camp source establishes how long teachers protect the block while the routine is being taught.

Days 1–3 45 minutes 7:45-8:30 Explicitly teach the why, model every move, and run practice do-overs.
Days 4–5 40 minutes 7:45-8:25 Continue explicit teaching while students begin carrying roles and commitments.
Days 6–8 30 minutes 7:45-8:15 Refine regulation, transitions, repair, and student leadership.

Normal master-schedule destination: Kindergarten–7th grade and DREAM 1–2 hold 7:45-8:05; Grade 8 holds 7:45-8:00. During Culture Camp, Days 1–8 intentionally continue past the K–7/DREAM endpoint, and Days 1–9 continue past the Grade 8 endpoint. Day 9 lands exactly at 8:05; Day 10 lands exactly at 8:00. That is the teaching plan, not a schedule error.

Rubric Shorthand

Use these rubric codes when planning, observing, or coaching:

  • R1 Regulation and Self-Management
  • R2 Orientation and Agency
  • R3 Voice and Community
  • R4 Practice and Skill Building
  • R5 Accountability and Leadership
  • R6 Restorative Response

Adult Deployment

  • If two adults are present, use Lead Adult for direct teaching and Support Adult for modeling, outlier support, materials, and live corrections.
  • If only one adult is present, collapse both roles into one lane and shorten the number of spoken directions.
  • Student leaders can begin carrying pieces of the routine on Day 4, but adult accountability remains active all 10 days.

How To Read The Script Tables

  • Block Minute means elapsed time inside that day’s Culture Camp lesson.
  • Clock windows begin at 7:45 and end at the protected scaffold time for that day.
  • Follow the six moves in order: Regulate, Orient, Connect, Practice, Commit, and Transition.
  • The daily shift is the emphasis, the amount of adult narration, and the degree of student ownership.
  • Teacher language should stay calm, direct, and repeatable.
  • If the Culture Camp routine needs a reset, insert it immediately inside the protected lesson window.

Day 1: Full-System Launch

Adult scaffold posture: heavy modeling, full narration, immediate stop-and-rebuild
Priority reset focus: the six-move sequence, Culture Promise language, and a clean transition into learning

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 45 minutes · 7:45-8:30. Explicitly teach the why, model every move, and make room for do-overs before releasing students into the academic block.

Day 1 Culture Camp Minute Map

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Lead adult script and action Support adult role Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 0:00-5:00 Regulate. Say, "Good morning. Find your spot. Eyes to the board. We are starting on the one. Let’s take three collective breaths." Model calm, open posture and wait for quiet. Use proximity or a quiet hand signal without interrupting the breathing exercise. Sit in the assigned meeting spot, face the board, and participate in breathing. R1
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 5:00-15:00 Orient. Say, "At Matchbook, we believe culture is practiced before pressure. This is our practice field. Today’s focus value is Safe. Safe means we keep ourselves and others from physical and emotional harm." Point to the visual schedule and give one concrete example if students need the meaning clarified. Listen, track the teacher, and read the board. R2, R3
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 15:00-25:00 Connect. Say, "Partner A, answer this: What helps you feel safe at school? Partner B, your job is just to listen. You have 30 seconds. Go." Coach partner spacing and reset the volume with the standard attention signal if needed. Share using the low-stakes prompt and practice active listening. R2, R3, R4
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 25:00-35:00 Practice. Say, "Safe means keeping hands and feet to ourselves. Let’s practice our line-up transition. When I say ‘Go,’ stand up safely without touching anyone’s desk. Go." Model the safe move and return students to the starting point for a clean do-over when needed. Practice standing, pushing in chairs safely, and repeating the transition until it is clean. R3, R4, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 35:00-40:00 Commit. Say, "Today I will be Safe by…" and have students whisper a specific commitment to a partner. Coach vague commitments into visible actions and record examples as needed. State one specific, actionable commitment. R2, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 40:00-45:00 Transition. Say, "Our value is Safe. Our first task is the Do Now. Stand on the one." Watch the transition, support outliers quietly, and note any step that needs tomorrow’s practice. Reply, "On the one," and move to the desk silently. R1, R4, R5

Day 2: Listening and Speaking Norms

Adult scaffold posture: strong adult lead with less explaining
Priority reset focus: listening body, sentence frames, and faster response to cues

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 45 minutes · 7:45-8:30. Continue explicit teaching and full rehearsal; this is not the 15-minute mastery routine yet.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 0:00-5:00 Lead breathing, then say, "Our bodies are ready. Now our ears and voices need to be ready too." Regulate and prepare to listen. R1
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 5:00-15:00 Say, "At Matchbook, questions belong here when they help learning move forward, but we protect each other's dignity." Point to sentence frames on the board. Track the sentence frames and repeat them aloud. R2, R3
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 15:00-25:00 Model the frames: "I agree with __ because..." "I want to add on..." "I disagree because..." "Can you explain...?" Practice the frames with a partner using a low-stakes prompt. R3, R4
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 25:00-35:00 Coach the listening body: "Face the speaker. Hands still. Wait until the speaker is done. Now try it again." Rehearse listening and speaking with cleaner turn-taking. R3, R4, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 35:00-40:00 Say, "Your commitment today should name one listening move you will actually do." Record a few examples. Make a commitment tied to listening or speaking. R2, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 40:00-45:00 Close with, "Take these same sentence frames into class discussion today. We do not switch cultures when academics begin." Transition to the next lesson and carry the language with them. R2, R5

Day 3: Body Language and Visible Respect

Adult scaffold posture: shorter directions, tighter practice loops
Priority reset focus: showing respect with bodies, not just words

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 45 minutes · 7:45-8:30. Keep the full teaching window while students learn ownership, visible respect, and repair after routine mistakes.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 0:00-5:00 Lead regulation and say, "Calm bodies help our class feel safe." Regulate and settle more quickly. R1
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 5:00-15:00 Say, "The Matchbook way means we show respect with our bodies, not just our words." Demonstrate facing the speaker versus slouching away. Watch the contrast and identify the stronger example. R2, R3
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 15:00-25:00 Give a short partner share and narrate: "Listeners, your job is visible now. Eyes on the speaker. Shoulders turned. Hands quiet." Share and listen while holding the expected posture. R3, R4
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 25:00-35:00 Run a do-over: "That was halfway there. Reset. This time show me respect before anyone speaks." Repeat the share with stronger body language. R3, R4, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 35:00-40:00 Prompt commitments: "Name one body move you will use today to show respect." Make a specific commitment tied to posture or attention. R2, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:30 40:00-45:00 Say, "We leave the circle with the same body control we used inside it." Transition to the next lesson with minimal extra talk. Move into learning while holding the same posture expectations. R1, R5

Day 4: Voice Tone and Early Student Roles

Adult scaffold posture: cue, observe, and give short resets
Priority reset focus: tone, role clarity, and protecting dignity during interaction

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 40 minutes · 7:45-8:25. Release five minutes while students begin carrying Speaker and Listener roles.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:25 0:00-5:00 Run regulation with less narration and name the first two roles: "Today we begin Speaker and Listener roles." Regulate and notice the role assignments. R1, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:25 5:00-15:00 Say, "At Matchbook, we protect each other's dignity with our tone." Model the same phrase in a respectful tone and a disrespectful tone. Identify which tone protects dignity. R2, R3
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:25 15:00-25:00 Let the Speaker read the prompt and the Listener restate the norm. Coach if needed, but do not take the role back too quickly. Begin carrying the prompt and the listening role with support. R3, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:25 25:00-35:00 Practice with a role-play: "Say, 'Can I use that marker?' once in a neutral tone and once in a rude tone. Which version belongs here?" Role-play the same words with different tones and reflect. R3, R4
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:25 35:00-40:00 Commit and Transition. Say, "Commit to using a neutral tone today. Carry the same tone into your first group interaction. Stand on the one." Make a commitment tied to voice tone, then transition while preserving the expectation. R1, R2, R5

Day 5: The Power of Commitments

Adult scaffold posture: coaching more than narrating
Priority reset focus: making commitments specific, visible, and usable during the day

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 40 minutes · 7:45-8:25. Students practice specific commitments while adults reduce narration without rushing the teaching.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:25 0:00-10:00 Regulate and Orient. Say, "Today we focus on commitments. A commitment is a promise to yourself. At Matchbook, we own our next step." Contrast a vague wish with a specific, visible action. Breathe, track the board, and compare vague versus specific examples. R1, R2, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:25 10:00-25:00 Connect and Practice. Ask, "What is the difference between ‘I will be good’ and ‘I will raise my hand’?" Have partners discuss and sort examples by whether the action is observable. Discuss how specificity creates accountability and identify commitments adults could actually see. R2, R3, R4, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:25 25:00-40:00 Commit and Transition. Say, "Write a specific, observable commitment. You will see it again before dismissal. Then we transition on the one." Write or post the commitment, revise it until it is visible, and transition knowing it will be checked later. R1, R2, R5

Day 6: Regulation Toolbox and Transitions

Adult scaffold posture: lighter prompts, sharper resets
Priority reset focus: students using regulation tools and carrying them into transitions

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 30 minutes · 7:45-8:15. The deep teaching of the why is complete; refine regulation tools and transitions.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 0:00-10:00 Regulate and Orient. Practice finger breathing and 5-4-3-2-1 grounding. Say, "These are tools, not punishments. The sequence is the intervention." Practice both tools, notice which one helps most, and connect regulation to a clean transition. R1, R2, R4
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 10:00-20:00 Connect and Practice. Ask which tool students may need after lunch or recess, then practice standing, pushing in, and moving silently. Share a likely regulation need and rehearse the transition until it improves. R1, R2, R3, R4, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 20:00-30:00 Commit and Transition. Say, "Commit to a smooth transition. You have 60 seconds to get to your seat and start the Do Now. Go." Commit to a regulation tool and transition to the desk in under 60 seconds, using a do-over if needed. R1, R2, R5

Day 7: Repair Language and Recovery

Adult scaffold posture: coach repair without dropping structure
Priority reset focus: routine errors, relational harm, and fast recovery

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 30 minutes · 7:45-8:15. Use the released time to practice neutral repair language and recovery.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 0:00-10:00 Regulate and Orient. Say, "We all make mistakes. Today we learn how to fix them using Regulate, Relate, Reason. At Matchbook, repair happens after regulation." Regulate and distinguish a routine error from relational harm. R1, R2, R6
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 10:00-20:00 Connect and Practice. Say, "Routine error is forgetting a pencil. Harm is hurting a feeling. Let’s role-play a quick ‘My bad.’ No long speeches. No excuses. Repair, then return." Role-play a routine error, neutral repair, and the difference between quick recovery and relational repair. R3, R4, R6
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 20:00-30:00 Commit and Transition. Say, "Commit to using repair language instead of arguing today. Repair is part of the routine, not outside of it. Stand on the one." Commit to accountable recovery and use "My bad" if a routine error occurs during transition. R2, R5, R6

Day 8: Student Leadership Without Loss of Fidelity

Adult scaffold posture: step back, but do not lower the bar
Priority reset focus: student-led ownership, houses and identity layered in without weakening the routine

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 30 minutes · 7:45-8:15. Students take on leadership while adults facilitate the facilitators.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 0:00-10:00 Regulate and Orient. Hand the routine roles to students and say, "I am stepping back today. I am watching whether the routine survives with your ownership." Student leaders open the board and values while the class responds with structure. R1, R2, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 10:00-20:00 Connect and Practice. Let student leaders run the share and cue the practice move. Coach quietly from the side only if voice protection or structure breaks. Run the share and practice portions with student ownership while preserving fidelity. R3, R4, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:15 20:00-30:00 Commit and Transition. Have a student leader summarize commitments and cue the move. Close with, "Leadership is real if the transition stays clean." State commitments, cue the transition, and move into learning with less adult talk. R1, R2, R5

Day 9: Recognition Through Evidence

Adult scaffold posture: layer celebration without softening expectations
Priority reset focus: keeping structure high on a high-energy day

Protected Culture Camp lesson: 20 minutes · 7:45-8:05. The system carries most of the routine while students practice evidence-based recognition.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:05 0:00-5:00 Regulate and Orient. Say, "High energy still needs structure. Today we talk about how we celebrate each other on Fridays. At Matchbook, recognition is evidence, not popularity." Regulate and track the criteria for fair recognition. R1, R2, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:05 5:00-15:00 Connect and Practice. Ask students to name an observed Safe, Respectful, or Responsible action, then write a nomination using evidence they actually saw. Share observed evidence and practice an evidence-based nomination. R3, R4, R5
Culture Camp lesson · 7:45-8:05 15:00-20:00 Commit and Transition. Say, "Commit to catching someone being Safe, Respectful, or Responsible today. If the routine falls apart, the celebration waits." Commit to observing peers and transition with expectations still high. R2, R5

Day 10: Readiness Review and Launch

Adult scaffold posture: observe more, intervene only when needed
Priority reset focus: the routines that are still too dependent on adult narration

Culture Camp mastery check: 15 minutes · 7:45-8:00. Start the timer, do not stop to reteach inside the routine, and evaluate readiness for the year-round schedule.

Culture Camp clock window Block Minute Teacher script and action Students do Rubric
Culture Camp mastery check · 7:45-8:00 0:00-2:00 Run regulation with the fewest possible words. Watch for whether students settle on the first cue. Regulate with minimal adult support. R1
Culture Camp mastery check · 7:45-8:00 2:00-4:00 Let students carry the values and the opening language as much as possible. Name the values and the purpose of the routine. R2, R5
Culture Camp mastery check · 7:45-8:00 4:00-7:00 Observe the share rather than over-coaching it. Only interrupt if the norms collapse. Share and listen with near-independent discipline. R3, R5
Culture Camp mastery check · 7:45-8:00 7:00-10:00 Let students lead the practice move. Note whether they can self-correct after a first miss. Practice with lighter adult intervention. R4, R5
Culture Camp mastery check · 7:45-8:00 10:00-13:00 Ask for commitments and listen for specificity without rewriting every answer for them. State launch-ready commitments with more agency. R2, R5
Culture Camp mastery check · 7:45-8:00 13:00-15:00 Close with, "Show me whether this routine lives beyond this circle." Observe the transition into learning. Transition into work with minimal help. R1, R5

Leader and Coach Read-Through

When observing the protected Culture Camp lesson, leaders and coaches should be able to answer these questions with low-inference evidence:

  • Did the teacher protect the full Culture Camp time assigned to that day?
  • Did the lesson move through Regulate, Orient, Connect, Practice, Commit, and Transition?
  • Did the teacher stop, reset, and repractice when a Culture Camp move broke?
  • Did student ownership increase over the ten days without lowering fidelity expectations?
  • Did the teacher connect observations to the rubric codes and a specific next-day reteach decision?
