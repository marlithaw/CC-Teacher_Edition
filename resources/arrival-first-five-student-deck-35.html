From the Door to Working

The Five Steps

1 · Enter

Enter calmly at voice 0–1. Greet back — you were greeted by name.

2 · Sit

Move directly to your seat. No detours, no clusters.

3 · Materials

Notebook, pencil, and today’s task materials out.

4 · Start

Begin the Do Now within 60 seconds.

5 · Sustain

Work independently for the full First Five.
